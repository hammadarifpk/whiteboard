1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Kervoxa ("we", "us", or "our") provides Whiteboard - Draw & Learn (the "App"). This policy explains how the App accesses, processes, stores, and shares information, including screen content, audio, imported files, and information handled by optional third-party services.

The App does not require an account. We do not operate a server that receives your boards or recordings. We do not sell personal information, display advertising, or use your board content for advertising or profiling. The App has no separate advertising, developer analytics, or crash-reporting service. Optional Google components have their own technical data handling, explained in section 4.

2. Information processed on your device

  • Board content: drawings, handwriting, text, sticky notes, shapes, images, imported document content, and other objects you place on a board. These are processed to display, edit, save, reopen, and export your work.
  • Library information: board names, folders, thumbnails, bookmarks, timestamps, and trash status help organize and restore your boards.
  • Preferences: theme, drawing and touch options, toolbar preferences, and other settings are saved locally to remember your choices.
  • Selected files: the App reads images, PDFs, and backup files that you choose through Android's file selection or sharing features. A copy or derived content may become part of a locally stored board.
  • Recordings: screen video and any audio you enable are saved locally as described below.

The App does not request access to your contacts, precise location, SMS, call logs, or camera. Importing a file does not give us access to the rest of your device's files.

3. Screen recording, microphone, and permissions

Recording is optional and starts only after you start it and approve Android's screen-capture request. Depending on Android's capture selection, a recording may include the selected app or other content visible on your screen, including other apps and sensitive information they display.

  • Screen capture / media projection: captures visible screen content to create the video you request. Android asks for capture consent for each recording session.
  • Microphone: used only when you select microphone audio or both microphone and internal audio, and grant audio permission. It can capture your voice and nearby sounds.
  • Internal audio: captures eligible device playback when you choose internal audio or both sources. Android and the source app control which playback can be captured.
  • Foreground services: the media-projection service keeps a recording running, and the microphone service supports your chosen microphone capture. Capture may continue while you move away from the App until you stop it or Android ends it.
  • Notifications: used to show recording status and a stop action, subject to your Android notification settings. Android also provides its own recording indicators and controls.

You can stop recording using the App's recording controls or available Android/notification controls. You can revoke microphone and notification permissions in Android Settings. Drawing does not require recording or microphone access.

The App does not upload, stream, or send your screen video or audio to Kervoxa, Wikimedia, or Google for recognition, analytics, or advertising. Recordings are stored in app storage and, where supported, saved to your device's media library. Exporting or sharing a recording is your choice.

4. Optional online tools and third-party services

Basic drawing and local board editing work without online tools. Optional online features are initially off and require you to enable them. You can turn them off in the App's privacy settings. Disabling them does not delete information already received by a provider or necessarily remove previously downloaded models.

Wikimedia Commons image search

When you search for online images, the App sends your search words to Wikimedia Commons and requests image thumbnails or downloads that you select. Wikimedia and its image delivery infrastructure receive your IP address and ordinary request details, including the requested resource and an App-identifying user agent. Your complete board, local files, and recordings are not sent as part of this search. Avoid putting private information into search terms.

Wikimedia handles these requests under the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.

Google ML Kit recognition and on-device summaries

The App uses Google ML Kit for optional image-text and handwriting recognition. On supported devices, summarization uses ML Kit and Android AICore/Gemini Nano. Recognition and summary content is processed on your device; the App does not send board images, ink strokes, or summary text to a cloud inference service. Language or feature models may need to be downloaded from Google.

Google's SDKs may collect technical information: device model, manufacturer, Android version, app package/version, installation or other identifiers (depending on the component), feature configuration and languages, input/output sizes, performance, errors, and events such as model downloads and API use. Google uses this for diagnostics, usage measurement, service improvement, and abuse prevention. Network requests also expose your IP address to the receiving service. This is distinct from uploading your board content.

See ML Kit data disclosures, ML Kit privacy terms, ML Kit GenAI terms, and the Google Privacy Policy. Providers control their own retention and international processing of information they receive.

5. When information is shared

Information may leave your device in the following circumstances:

  • You export, back up, or share: the selected board, image, PDF, backup, or recording is sent to the destination you choose. A cloud storage provider, messaging app, email service, or recipient may receive and retain it under their own policies. Android grants the selected receiving app access needed for the share.
  • You use optional online tools: Wikimedia receives image-search requests, and Google handles model downloads and SDK technical information as described above.
  • You transfer devices: supported Android device-to-device transfer can move app data to your new device. The App's backup rules exclude its private data from Android automatic cloud backup. Exported files and recordings in shared storage may separately be backed up by services you enable.
  • You contact us: Kervoxa and its email provider receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use them to answer questions, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests. Send only information necessary for your request.

We may disclose information we actually hold, such as support correspondence, where required by applicable law or necessary to address fraud, abuse, or security incidents. This does not give us remote access to boards or recordings stored on your device.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Boards: retained locally until you delete them or remove the App's data. Deleted boards enter Trash, where you can restore them or delete them permanently. The App's cleanup removes boards that have remained in Trash for more than 30 days when cleanup runs.
  • App data: clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling removes its private local data, subject to Android's behavior. Save any work you want to keep first.
  • Exports, backups, and recordings: copies saved outside private app storage may remain after a board is deleted or the App is uninstalled. Delete these separately through your files, gallery, backup provider, or receiving service.
  • Models and third-party records: downloaded models may remain in storage managed by the App or Google components. Google and Wikimedia determine retention of their own service records; consult their policies and privacy controls.
  • Support messages: retained only as necessary to resolve the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed.

There is no App account to delete. Kervoxa cannot retrieve or remotely delete your locally stored boards or copies already shared with another party. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection concerning personal information we hold, or to complain to a relevant privacy authority. Contact us to make a request; we may need to verify it.

7. Security

Private app files rely on Android's app sandbox and device security. App-managed web requests use HTTPS, and Google documents HTTPS protection for ML Kit technical data in transit. The App does not add password protection or its own encryption to exported documents, backup archives, or recordings. Protect your device and choose sharing destinations carefully. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not require a name, birth date, or account to draw. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through support communications. A parent or guardian should supervise online tools, screen/audio recording, and sharing where appropriate. If you believe a child has sent personal information to Kervoxa, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Local drawings remain under the control of the device user or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The revised policy will show a new last-updated date. Where required, material changes will also be explained in the App and any necessary consent obtained before the changed processing begins.
